The website already has the expertise. My job is to make sure Google can find it, understand it and connect it with the right U.S. searches.

What I found

The first job is not more content.

The current audit points to indexing, duplication, canonical and performance issues. If those signals are unclear, publishing more pages simply gives Google more URLs to process before the foundation is fixed.

01 / Indexation

Google needs cleaner signals.

I’ll sort the sitemap, excluded URLs, redirects, canonicals, orphan pages and unnecessary WordPress URLs so the right pages are easier to crawl and index.

02 / Performance

Mobile speed needs attention.

The audit shows JavaScript/main-thread and mobile performance issues. I’ll focus on the parts that materially affect Core Web Vitals and user experience.

03 / Search relevance

The services need clearer search targets.

Key pages should each have a clear commercial intent. That gives Leave a Mark a better chance to rank for the specific services U.S. buyers are actually searching for.

90-Day roadmap

Fix. Optimise. Grow.

The order matters. I want the site technically clean before we ask Google to rank more commercial pages. Then I want enough live data to see what is actually working before deciding what to scale.

Month 01

Fix the foundation.

Make it easier for Google to crawl, index and measure the site correctly.

  • Search Console and indexation clean-up
  • Sitemap, redirects, 404s, noindex and canonical fixes
  • English/Hebrew duplication and hreflang review
  • Full WordPress technical crawl
  • Core Web Vitals and mobile performance work
  • Keyword, traffic and conversion baseline
What I want to see

Fewer crawl/indexing issues, cleaner URL signals, better performance and a reliable baseline before the growth work starts.

Month 02

Improve commercial relevance.

Match the site more closely with the way potential U.S. clients search for these services.

  • U.S. keyword and competitor research
  • Core service-page optimisation
  • High-intent landing pages where the data supports them
  • Internal links between services, content and case studies
  • Trust, author and expertise signals
  • Structured data clean-up and validation
What I want to see

More relevant non-branded impressions, new keyword visibility, stronger snippets and early movement on long-tail commercial terms.

Month 03

Push what is gaining traction.

Use real Search Console data instead of guessing which pages or topics deserve the next round of work.

  • Queries with impressions but weak clicks
  • Keywords sitting around positions 5–20
  • Content cluster and existing-blog optimisation
  • Relevant authority and backlink opportunities
  • Conversion improvements on priority pages
  • Final crawl and before-vs-after review
What I want to see

Wider keyword coverage, stronger U.S. visibility and a clear list of the pages and themes worth investing in next.

Month 01

Technical SEO first.

I’m putting the technical work first because crawlability and indexation affect everything that comes after it. There is little value in optimising or creating pages that Google is struggling to process correctly.

01

Search Console & indexation

Review sitemap processing, Crawled — Currently Not Indexed pages, 404s, redirected URLs, noindex pages, robots restrictions, Google-selected canonicals and unnecessary exclusions.

02

WordPress technical crawl

Check status codes, redirect chains, canonicals, crawl depth, internal redirects, broken links, duplicate URLs, thin utility pages and Elementor/page-builder output.

03

English / Hebrew SEO

Review /he/ URLs, duplicated language pages, self-referencing canonicals, reciprocal hreflang, x-default where appropriate and language-specific sitemap URLs.

04

Performance & Core Web Vitals

Optimise images, LCP assets, JavaScript, unused CSS, fonts, third-party scripts, caching and heavy Elementor output.

LCP ≤ 2.5sINP ≤ 200msCLS ≤ 0.10
05

Measurement baseline

Track clicks, impressions, average position, indexed pages, non-branded visibility, U.S. performance, priority keywords, landing pages and conversions where access is available.

Month 03

Let Search Console tell us what to do next.

By Month 3 I should have enough data to stop treating every page equally. The quickest opportunities are often the queries where Google is already showing the site but the page is not yet earning enough clicks or ranking strongly enough.

Expected results

No ranking promises. Clear progress markers.

SEO rankings are controlled by Google and competitors keep changing, so I will not promise a specific position, traffic percentage or number of leads. I will measure the work against the signals we should reasonably expect to improve at each stage.

0–30

Get the site cleaner.

  • Better crawlability
  • Cleaner indexation
  • Fewer technical errors
  • Correct sitemap signals
  • Better website performance
  • Reliable tracking
30–60

Look for early search movement.

  • More relevant impressions
  • New U.S. keyword visibility
  • More non-branded appearances
  • Priority pages indexed
  • Early ranking movement
  • Better landing-page engagement
60–90

Find the areas worth scaling.

  • Wider keyword coverage
  • Stronger selected rankings
  • More U.S. organic visibility
  • More qualified visitors
  • More enquiry opportunities
  • Clear next-phase priorities
Important

Competitive commercial keywords can take longer than 90 days. The purpose of the first three months is to build a clean foundation, create relevance, show measurable movement and identify where the best long-term growth is likely to come from.
