Analgesic Healthcare

Healthcare Website Management, SEO & Digital Marketing Support

An ongoing digital support role combining WordPress website management, SEO, content publishing, technical troubleshooting, security, social media, and marketing assistance within one connected workflow.

WordPress Management SEO Content Publishing Digital Marketing Support
MY ROLE Website Manager, SEO Specialist & Virtual Marketing Assistant

I work as an extension of the organisation's internal team, supporting the website, SEO, content, technical maintenance, social media, and day-to-day digital marketing requirements.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Managing the website as an active digital marketing platform.

I provide ongoing website management, SEO, content, and digital marketing support for a healthcare organisation, working as an extension of the internal team rather than simply handling isolated website tasks.

The objective is to keep the organisation's website accurate, secure, optimised, and continuously improving while supporting its wider digital marketing activities.

The role brings together website management, SEO, content publishing, AI-assisted content workflows, troubleshooting, security, social media, video, and marketing support so each update works as part of a larger digital ecosystem.

THE CHALLENGE

Healthcare websites need continuous quality, trust, and technical care.

Healthcare websites require more than occasional content updates. The website needs to remain professional, trustworthy, technically healthy, search-friendly, secure, and easy to manage while new content is regularly being published.

At the same time, the organisation requires ongoing marketing support across multiple channels. This means every new page, article, campaign, or website update needs to work as part of a larger digital ecosystem rather than being treated as a standalone task.

Professional presentation Technical health Search visibility Security Content consistency Marketing coordination
WEBSITE MANAGEMENT

Day-to-day WordPress management without compromising consistency.

I manage ongoing WordPress website requirements and make sure new content can be added without compromising the quality or consistency of the existing site.

  • Creating new website pages
  • Updating existing pages
  • Uploading and formatting content
  • Maintaining layouts and branding
  • Adding images and media
  • Improving page structure
  • Checking responsive behaviour
  • Updating calls-to-action
  • Fixing layout and functionality issues

Each update is reviewed from both a user experience and SEO perspective before it goes live.

CONTENT & BLOG WORKFLOW

Turning simple uploads into structured, search-optimised content.

I manage the complete blog publishing workflow, including preparing content, formatting articles inside WordPress, improving readability, optimising images, adding internal links, and configuring SEO information.

Headline structure H1/H2/H3 hierarchy Keyword placement Meta titles Meta descriptions URL structure Internal linking Image alt text Readability Calls-to-action Mobile formatting
AI-ASSISTED CONTENT CREATION

Using AI as a production assistant, with human review at the centre.

I use AI tools to support research, ideation, drafting, and optimisation, but content is not simply generated and published.

I review and refine AI-assisted content so it matches the organisation's tone, reads naturally, has a clear structure, avoids unnecessary repetition, supports search intent, connects to relevant services, and provides genuine value to the reader.

SEO MANAGEMENT

Ongoing on-page and technical SEO alongside website management.

  • Keyword and content optimisation
  • Meta title and description optimisation
  • Internal linking
  • Image SEO
  • Heading structure
  • Indexing checks
  • Sitemap management
  • Broken link monitoring
  • Redirect management
  • Technical SEO checks
  • Website health monitoring
  • Updating older content

The approach includes improving existing content as well as publishing new material, helping build a more sustainable SEO process over time.

QA, TROUBLESHOOTING & SECURITY

Keeping the live site reliable while it continues to evolve.

Every important website update goes through visual, technical, and SEO quality assurance. I also investigate and resolve many day-to-day website issues directly rather than requiring a separate developer for every small technical problem.

Desktop QA Tablet responsiveness Mobile responsiveness Typography & spacing Buttons & links Forms Navigation Page loading Plugin conflicts Bug fixing WordPress updates Security checks Vulnerability monitoring Backups Post-update QA
SOCIAL MEDIA & DIGITAL MARKETING

Extending website content into wider marketing activity.

My involvement goes beyond the website. I also support the organisation with day-to-day digital marketing requirements, including social media graphics, campaign content, captions, promotional posts, educational content, short-form video, and marketing visuals.

This allows website content to be repurposed across other marketing channels rather than existing in isolation. One article can become a website post, social media graphic, short video, caption, or campaign asset.

THE SOLUTION

The value is continuity across the organisation's digital operation.

The biggest value I provide to this project is not one individual deliverable. It is continuity.

The organisation has one person who understands the website, its content, its SEO requirements, its marketing activities, and its technical setup.

Instead of coordinating separately with a website developer, WordPress manager, SEO specialist, content uploader, writer, social media assistant, video editor, and technical support person, these responsibilities can be handled or coordinated within one ongoing workflow.

  • A repeatable blog publishing process
  • Stronger SEO foundations

  • Regularly updated marketing content
