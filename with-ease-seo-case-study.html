MY ROLE SEO Specialist & Website Optimization Expert

I worked on diagnosing and resolving Google indexing issues, improving the technical SEO foundation, optimising on-page SEO, improving analytics tracking, and developing a Dutch-language SEO strategy for the client's Squarespace website.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Building a healthier search foundation for a holistic therapy brand in Amsterdam.

I helped With Ease, a holistic therapy brand based in Amsterdam, address major Google Search Console and indexing issues that were affecting the website's search visibility.

The project focused on identifying the technical problems preventing Google from correctly understanding and indexing the website, while also improving the broader SEO foundation for future organic growth.

Google Search Console troubleshooting Indexing issue resolution Sitemap analysis Canonical investigation Technical SEO On-page SEO Local SEO Analytics tracking Dutch-language SEO strategy Squarespace optimisation
THE CHALLENGE

Technical investigation had to work within the existing Squarespace environment.

  • Pages not being indexed correctly
  • Google Search Console indexing errors
  • Sitemap-related issues
  • Canonical URL problems
  • Weak on-page SEO
  • Local search visibility opportunities
  • Analytics tracking that needed improvement
  • The need for a more structured Dutch-language SEO strategy

Because the website was built with Squarespace, the solution needed to work within the platform's technical limitations and existing structure.

TECHNICAL SEO & INDEXING WORK

Diagnosing why Google was struggling to process important pages.

Google Search Console Audit

I reviewed Google Search Console to identify indexing and technical issues affecting the website and determine which problems required direct fixes.

Sitemap Issues

I investigated sitemap-related errors to help Google properly discover important website URLs and create a cleaner relationship between the site structure, sitemap and crawling.

Canonical Issues

Canonical URL problems were reviewed to help search engines understand which version of a page should be treated as the primary URL.

Indexing Optimisation

I worked on resolving indexing problems so important website pages had a stronger chance of being correctly discovered and included in Google's index.

ON-PAGE SEO OPTIMISATION

Improving how individual pages communicate relevance.

Alongside the technical fixes, I reviewed the website's on-page SEO to improve how individual pages communicated their relevance to both search engines and potential customers.

Page optimisation Search relevance SEO structure Metadata Content targeting Local search intent
LOCAL SEO & DUTCH-LANGUAGE STRATEGY

Connecting stronger technical foundations with local search relevance.

Because With Ease operates as a holistic therapy brand in Amsterdam, local search visibility was an important part of the project. The SEO work therefore considered how potential clients searching for relevant therapy and wellness services in Amsterdam could discover the business organically.

I also developed an SEO direction specifically around Dutch-language search opportunities to help the website target relevant local searches more effectively while maintaining alignment with the client's services and audience.

ANALYTICS & TRACKING

Creating a stronger foundation for future SEO decisions.

Analytics tracking was reviewed and improved as part of the broader website optimisation work. This created a stronger foundation for understanding organic visibility, search behaviour, website performance and future SEO opportunities.

Technical troubleshooting combined with long-term SEO strategy.
