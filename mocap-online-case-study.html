Mocap Online

Shopify SEO Optimisation – Technical Fixes & Growth Strategy

A Shopify technical SEO project focused on resolving critical issues, improving Semrush Site Health, restoring important functionality, and creating a clear roadmap for long-term organic growth.

Shopify Technical SEO SEO Audit Error Resolution Growth Strategy
MY ROLE Shopify SEO Specialist

Responsible for auditing the Shopify website, resolving technical SEO issues, improving site health, fixing functional problems affecting customers, reviewing Semrush findings, and creating a long-term organic growth roadmap.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Technical SEO remediation for a large Shopify catalogue.

Mocap Online provides motion capture animation assets for animators, game developers, and creative studios worldwide. The Shopify store had a large product catalogue but was experiencing search visibility problems, technical SEO errors, and functionality issues that were affecting the user experience.

I was brought in to identify and resolve the most important technical SEO problems highlighted through Semrush while ensuring that changes would not introduce unnecessary risks to the existing Shopify store.

The project combined technical SEO remediation, metadata improvements, broken-link fixes, Shopify functionality troubleshooting, reporting, and planning for future organic growth.

MAIN CHALLENGES

The issues affecting both SEO performance and customer experience.

  • Declining Semrush Site Health score
  • 404 errors
  • Broken internal links
  • Duplicate title tags
  • Duplicate meta descriptions
  • Invalid structured data warnings
  • Indexing-related issues
  • Customer problems with invoice printing
  • Shopify Legacy account and Thank You page migration concerns
TECHNICAL SEO WORK

Core fixes completed during the project.

  • 404 Errors: Reviewed affected URLs and submitted removal requests where appropriate. Status: Complete
  • Broken Internal Links: Inspected affected links and removed or replaced them where required. Status: Complete
  • Duplicate Title Tags: Reviewed affected pages and created unique titles. Status: Complete
  • Duplicate Meta Descriptions: Audited the affected URLs, organised the issues, and corrected the duplicated metadata. Status: Complete
  • Invoice PDF / Printing Issue: Investigated the issue and restored invoice-printing functionality by updating the subscription setup. Status: Complete

Supporting work completed alongside the technical fixes.

Broken links and redirects 404 review robots.txt cleanup sitemap.xml review Duplicate metadata corrections Semrush warning review False-positive issue review Indexing problem review Shopify migration guidance SEO error-fixing report Video walkthrough
