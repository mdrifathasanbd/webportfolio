PROJECT OVERVIEW Travel Website Redesign – Optimistic Fiji Tours & Transfers

I redesigned the website for Optimistic Fiji Tours & Transfers, a local travel and transportation business in Fiji. The project focused on both the visual experience and the traveller's journey through the website.

PROJECT CONTEXT

The original website shared information, but it did not guide travellers clearly enough.

The original website provided information about the company's services and destinations, but the overall experience did not guide visitors clearly enough toward making a booking decision.

The redesign therefore focused on both the visual experience and the traveller's journey through the website.

The core case study message: the goal was not just to make the website look better. It was to make it easier to trust, easier to explore, and easier to book.

THE CHALLENGE

Restructuring the experience around what a traveller needs to know first.

  • The overall design felt dated
  • The visual hierarchy was weak
  • The booking journey was not obvious enough
  • Destinations felt more like a basic gallery than something travellers could explore
  • Important information was not always presented in a decision-friendly way
  • The website did not strongly communicate the feeling of travelling in Fiji

The redesign needed to quickly answer four key user questions: Where can I go? What can I book? Why should I trust this company? and How do I reserve my ride?

HOMEPAGE REDESIGN

A clearer introduction to the business and a more guided path into booking.

The homepage was redesigned to provide a clearer introduction to the business while helping visitors move naturally toward exploring and booking.

  • Stronger travel-focused hero section
  • Clear booking and search interface
  • Visual service categories
  • Popular transfer routes
  • Destination cards
  • Trust signals and clearer calls to action
  • More structured content hierarchy
  • Improved visual storytelling

Intended user journey: Arrive → Understand the service → Explore options → Build trust → Start booking.

TRANSFERS PAGE REDESIGN

Helping travellers find the right transfer quickly and move toward booking.

The Transfers page was redesigned around one primary goal: help travellers find the right transfer quickly and move toward booking.

The experience was simplified to reduce unnecessary friction around route discovery. Travellers can immediately search using pickup location, destination, travel date, and number of passengers.

  • Search + booking flow
  • Popular route cards
  • Easy decision making
  • Less clutter
  • Clearer pricing
  • Better overall flow

Supporting content was structured around clear route cards, easy-to-scan information, more obvious booking actions, and cleaner browsing.

DESTINATIONS PAGE REDESIGN

Turning a simple gallery into a more useful and travel-focused discovery experience.

The original destinations experience felt more like a simple gallery. I redesigned it to make destination discovery more useful and travel-focused.

The goal was to help visitors understand where Optimistic Fiji Tours operates, which locations they can visit, what destinations are available, and how destination discovery connects naturally with transportation and booking.

  • Visual destination grid
  • Destination imagery and clear names
  • Coverage information
  • Travel-focused browsing
  • Route and search functionality
  • Stronger connection between discovery and booking

Intended outcome: Browse Faster → Discover Destinations → Build Travel Interest → Move Toward Booking.

VISUAL DIRECTION

Refreshing the website so it feels more connected to the Fiji travel experience.

Cleaner layouts More whitespace Stronger travel photography Improved typography Clearer hierarchy Consistent cards Consistent buttons Stronger booking CTAs Modern tropical identity Better page-to-page consistency

The visual language was designed to make the website feel more connected to the Fiji travel experience while maintaining clear usability.

UX STRATEGY

Moving the website from simply showing information to guiding visitors toward a decision.

The biggest change was not simply visual. The original website mainly showed information. The redesigned experience attempts to guide visitors toward a decision.

Instead of treating each page as a standalone collection of content, the redesign creates a clearer journey between Discovery → Trust → Service Selection → Booking.

The focus was on solving the business problem behind the visual design.

MAIN IMPROVEMENTS

What the redesign improved across the experience.

  • Clearer Booking Journey: booking-related actions are introduced earlier and presented more clearly.
  • Better Route Discovery: travellers can quickly identify available transfer options and relevant routes.
  • Destination Discovery: destinations are presented as part of the travel experience rather than as a simple image gallery.
  • Stronger Visual Hierarchy: important information and calls to action are easier to identify.
  • Travel-Focused Identity: photography, page structure, and presentation better reflect the experience of travelling in Fiji.
  • Simplified Decision Making: information is organised around what travellers need to know before booking.
SKILLS & DELIVERABLES

UX, UI, booking flow thinking, and travel-focused redesign work.
