MY PROCESS

A clear process from first questions to final launch.

I keep each stage simple and collaborative, so you always know what is happening, what comes next, and where your feedback fits in.

01 Clarify

Discover & Clarify

I learn about your goals, audience, requirements and current challenges, then ask the right questions to make the scope clear.

02 Direction

Strategy & Mockup

I plan the sitemap, user journey and design direction. When useful, I create mockups first so the visual direction is agreed early.

03 Build

Design & Develop

I turn the approved direction into a responsive, fast and editable website with clean structure, strong UX and SEO in mind.

04 Refine

Review & Revise

You review the build and share feedback. I refine the design, content presentation and details until everything feels right.

05 Launch

Test & Launch

I complete final revisions, responsive checks, speed and SEO testing, then prepare the website for a smooth launch.
